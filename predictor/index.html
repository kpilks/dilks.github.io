How to install

  1. Tap the download button above on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. That's needed for apps that don't come from the Play Store.
  3. Open Prediction League, tap Register, and choose your team name. That's the name everyone sees in the league table.

How to play

  • Each week there are 10 matches. Pick Home, Draw or Away for each one.
  • Tap the ★ on one match to make it your banker. Get it right and you score double.
  • You can change your picks as often as you like until the deadline, which is normally the first kick-off.
  • Results are the 90-minute score. A match that goes to extra time or penalties counts as a draw.
  • Scores update live during matches, and the league table updates with them.
